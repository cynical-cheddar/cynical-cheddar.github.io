Fintech Simulation Education Engineer (2023)

I have experience developing a multiplayer, multi-agent framework for simulating a continuous double auction, in which bots and humans may compete against each other. 

The project is deployed at the University of Bristol's Financial Technology with Data Science MSc, where it serves as a teaching aid and forms part of their coursework.

The simulation emulates and expands upon the behaviour of Bristol Stock Exchange (BSE), a simple minimal simulation of a limit-order-book financial exchange, developed by Dave Cliff.

The application is written in C# .NET, utilising a Unity frontend for human-use and a Python API for bots.
